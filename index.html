01

General Guidelines

The standards expected from every member of the city.

Microphone Requirement

  • A working microphone is required in-city at all times.
  • Clear communication is essential for roleplay and in-game reporting.

Community Conduct

  • Staff are not responsible for mediating personal disputes between players.
  • Revenge or petty reporting is strictly prohibited.
  • Staff may remove players for OOC misconduct to maintain a safe, healthy community.

Mature Content

  • This is an 18+ server. Mature or adult humor and discussions may occur.
  • ERP is allowed, subject to applicable consent and server rules.

Respect & Harassment

  • Racism, sexism, and all forms of hateful prejudice are prohibited.
  • Harassment of any kind, sexual or otherwise, may result in a ban.

Appearance & Clothing

  • Realistic clothing is required.
  • Civilians may not wear PD or MD uniforms.
  • Public nudity is prohibited.

Staff Conduct

  • Constructive criticism is welcome.
  • Toxicity toward staff will not be tolerated.

Monetization Policy

  • Selling in-game items or services for real money is prohibited.
  • Trading in-game cash for real money is strictly forbidden.

Business & Property

  • Inactive businesses may be revoked by staff.
02

Character Creation

Create a character that belongs in the world.

Customization

  • All characters must be fully customized; default ped models are not allowed.
  • Staff may request changes if a character lacks effort.

Names & Models

  • Use common sense and keep character names appropriate.
  • Character models should match the character's intended gender presentation.
  • LGBTQ+ characters are fully supported.
03

Roleplay Guidelines

Keep immersion strong and interactions meaningful.

Roleplay First

  • Stay in character at all times during active roleplay.
  • Report rule breaks after the scenario is complete, except serious violations requiring immediate intervention.
  • Criminals must reciprocate roleplay when interacting with PD.
  • Threatened characters must roleplay fear and compliance where appropriate.

OOC Discussions

  • Keep in-game conversations in character.
  • Do not break character to explain rules or threaten reports.
  • OOC phrases that break immersion, such as “come out the Safe Zone,” are prohibited.

Safe Zones

  • Protected areas include city event areas, apartments, hospitals, player-owned shops, and legal civilian job locations.
  • Violence or hostile roleplay is not allowed in safe zones.
04

Combat Rules

Fair play, realistic consequences, and no exploiting mechanics.

Combat Logging

  • Disconnecting during active roleplay to avoid consequences is prohibited.
  • Report crashes through the designated Discord crash-report channel.
  • Rejoin and continue the scene where feasible.

Item Returns

  • Submit a clip showing the lost items and relevant circumstances.
  • Evidence showing rule-breaking may void the item-return request.

Exploiting

  • Abusing game mechanics, bugs, or glitches for personal gain is prohibited.

Powergaming

  • Always allow counterplay for other players.
  • Unrealistic drug selling or NPC exploitation is prohibited.

Escorting & Carrying

  • Only one person may be carried at a time.
  • Use vehicles properly; escorted players must be safely inside when transported.

Metagaming

  • Using real-life or out-of-game information to gain an in-character advantage is prohibited.

RDM — Random Deathmatch

  • Attacking without valid roleplay is prohibited.
  • Revenge killing is forbidden.

VDM — Vehicle Deathmatch

  • Using vehicles to attack players without valid roleplay is prohibited.

Value Your Life (VoL)

  • Fear realistic threats and avoid unnecessary combat when your character's life is at risk.
05

Roleplay Reminders

Rules for scenarios, evidence, conflict, and consequences.

Interaction Duration

  • All interactions must provide meaningful roleplay.
  • Civilians cannot harass whitelisted factions without provocation or valid roleplay justification.

Bored RP

  • Avoid aimless criminal activity, including robbing or shooting without proper roleplay.

RP in Businesses

  • A minimum of two minutes of meaningful interaction is required before violence.
  • Employees must clearly indicate on-duty or off-duty status.
  • Robbing businesses without valid roleplay is prohibited.

Hostage Rules

  • Hostages must be random civilians, not faction members.
  • Option A: 2:1 ratio with quality interaction lasting 20–30 seconds.
  • Option B: 3:1 ratio, guns already drawn, minimal interaction.
  • Maximum two hostages per scene.
  • Killing a hostage is allowed only when justified by the roleplay.

Third-Partying

  • Interfering in ongoing scenarios or shootouts without valid involvement is prohibited.

Scamming RP

  • Scamming players during vehicle or house transactions is prohibited.

Robbing

  • Taking the majority of a player's items without appropriate roleplay is not allowed.

Ocean Dumping

  • Allowed for story purposes, but cannot force memory loss.
  • Vehicles may not be dumped without valid roleplay.

Recording & Evidence

  • Evidence videos must be captured using recording software.
  • Run clipping software when a scene starts.
  • Clips must be at least two minutes long.
  • Failure to clip may void item returns and in-game reports.
  • Reports must be submitted within 24 hours of the event.
  • Reports must include video and audio showing the full interaction and events leading to the alleged rule break. Evidence from both sides should be included when available and required.

Hitmen & Bounties

  • Allowed with prior knowledge and realistic roleplay.

Conflict Expiration

  • Conflicts must allow a way to end the roleplay.
  • Continual harassment without resolution may be considered griefing.

Character Kill-Off (CK)

  • Must be agreed upon by the affected player.

New Life Rule (NLR)

  • Players may not remember events leading to their death for 15 minutes.
  • Scene reminders must be roleplayed and clipped.
06

Fail Roleplay (Fail RP)

High-quality, realistic roleplay is expected at all times.

Examples of Fail RP

  • Ignoring or failing to roleplay injuries.
  • Using OOC information for in-character advantage.
  • Posting drug locations publicly without valid roleplay justification.
  • Impersonating PD or EMS.
  • Neglecting required /me commands.
  • Death-running or walking/running in circles to avoid consequences.
  • Spectating shootouts without legitimate involvement.
  • Cop baiting without meaningful roleplay.
07

PD Player Rules

Interactions with law enforcement must remain realistic.

Law Enforcement Interactions

  • Fear PD realistically.
  • Arrests must be respected; RDM and robbery of PD are prohibited.
  • Warning shots must be given before engaging PD in combat, subject to the circumstances and applicable server rules.
  • Invalid PD actions should be reported through DOJ cases.
  • Lethal force is allowed only when justified by roleplay and applicable server rules.
08

EMS Player Rules

Respect medical roleplay and emergency services.

Medical Roleplay

  • Spamming EMS calls is prohibited.
  • Press “G” or use /911ems for medical emergencies.
  • Scenes should be considered over once EMS arrives, unless further roleplay is required or authorized.
  • Roleplay injuries realistically; “taps” are not allowed.
  • EMS may deny service if disrespected, in accordance with server procedures.
  • Hospitals are safe zones, not general hangout spots.
